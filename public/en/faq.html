FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Another question? Write to us, we reply quickly.

How is Lyze paid?

Through a commission on rental income, taken only when the property is rented. The calculator gives you an estimate.

Which platforms is my property listed on?

Airbnb, Booking and Expedia. Every booking lands in a single calendar, visible in your owner area.

Can I block dates for myself?

Yes. Let us know and we close the dates on every platform.

Who takes care of cleaning and linen?

Our local team, between every stay: full cleaning and hotel-quality linen.

How do I follow my income?

Your owner area shows every booking, its platform and the amount you receive.

Which cities do you cover?

El Jadida, Casablanca and Marrakech. Somewhere else? Write to us, we look at every request.

Can I stop whenever I want?

Yes, with notice so that confirmed bookings are honoured.
