My story

A woman, her values
and a passion.

01

My journey

I

I am convinced that the most beautiful adventures often begin with a deep desire to give new meaning to one's life.

Before founding Lyze Conciergerie, I worked as a sales administration assistant. A career that taught me rigour, organisation and the importance of client relationships.

But I wanted to go further. To create something that truly reflects who I am, where I could combine my taste for beautiful things, my attention to detail and, above all, what has always driven me: human contact.

Of Moroccan origin, I naturally chose to turn to Morocco. A country I am deeply attached to, but also a country in full transformation, offering great opportunities for entrepreneurs.

Starting my own business was a big decision. As with any new adventure, there were doubts, questions and sometimes fears. But my determination and my desire to succeed were always stronger.

I wanted to build a company in my own image: human, elegant, demanding in the quality of its services and deeply committed to trust.

02  The name

A name with a story

LisaLyze

Lyze is not just a company name.

It is inspired by my daughter's name, Lisa. A way for me to bring together this professional project and what I hold most dear: my family.

This choice gives Lyze Conciergerie a very special meaning. Behind this company, there is a personal story, values and a great deal of heart.

03

My vision of concierge services

To me, concierge services are not just about managing bookings or welcoming guests.

Above all, it is a human relationship.

I know that entrusting your home to someone else is a big decision. That is why I place trust, transparency and listening at the heart of every collaboration.

  • 01Trust
  • 02Transparency
  • 03Listening

I love well-kept places, welcoming atmospheres and the small details that make all the difference. Every home deserves special attention, just as every owner deserves personalised support.

My ambition is not simply to manage properties, but to build lasting relationships, founded on respect, closeness and professionalism.

Today, through Lyze Conciergerie, present in Casablanca, El Jadida and Marrakech, I want to give owners the peace of mind they are looking for, while enhancing the value of their property.

I firmly believe that a company can be professional and demanding while remaining approachable, warm and human.

Because behind every home, there is an owner, a story and a trust that must be earned.

And it is with this philosophy that I chose to build Lyze Conciergerie.

“Because behind every home, there is an owner, a story and a trust that must be earned.”

— Barka, founder
