Our support in 5 steps

First call – Getting to know each other

A no-commitment phone call to understand your expectations, discover your property and set your goals together.

Visit and assessment of your property

We visit your property to assess its rental potential. If needed, we suggest targeted improvements to enhance your property and make it more attractive.

Tailored proposal and contract

We present a clear management offer suited to your needs. Once approved, we formalise our collaboration with a transparent contract.

Showcasing and going live

We prepare your property for rental, create attractive listings and publish them on the booking platforms.

Full management and income optimisation

We handle the day-to-day management of your rentals: bookings, guest check-in, stay follow-up, maintenance and coordination of services. We regularly adjust our pricing strategy to optimise your occupancy rate and rental income.
